About this map

This map compares two separately maintained hydrography datasets covering Washington State:

  • OpenStreetMap (OSM) waterways — crowd-sourced line features tagged as streams, rivers, canals, etc, extracted from the OSM planet dataset.
  • USGS National Hydrography Dataset (NHD) flowlines — an authoritative, professionally maintained hydrography dataset produced by the U.S. Geological Survey.

Note: the difference in terminology (waterways vs. flowlines) simply reflects what each organization calls these features in its source data.

Boundary layers (counties, and USGS HUC-8 and HUC-10 watersheds) let one select an area and compare stream mileage between the two datasets within it. Mileage statistics are precomputed offline from these two datasets and shown in the sidebar when a boundary is selected.
